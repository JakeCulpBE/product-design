# Product Design AI Guidelines

A central repository for product design AI MDs, guidance, patterns, and team standards.

## Purpose

This repo is the source of truth for how we design AI-powered product experiences. It brings together the principles, decision frameworks, documentation, and reusable guidance the team can use to create consistent, thoughtful, and high-quality work.

## What lives here

- Product design AI MDs
- Design principles and standards
- Interaction and UX guidance
- Prompting and conversation design patterns
- Accessibility and usability considerations
- Research notes and best practices
- Review checklists and decision frameworks

## Goals

- Create consistency across AI experiences
- Make guidance easy to find and reuse
- Reduce repeated decision-making
- Help teams design responsibly and clearly
- Support faster onboarding for designers and cross-functional partners

## Who this is for

- Product designers
- Content designers
- Product managers
- Researchers
- Engineers working on AI experiences
